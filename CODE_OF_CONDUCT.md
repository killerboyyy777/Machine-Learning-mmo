# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

This project has a dual audience, non-coder players and config tuners on one side and ML researchers on the other. Both are equally welcome. Beginners asking basic questions about `world.json`, the dashboard, or training runs receive the same respect as experienced contributors.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Accusations of cheating without evidence. Use the anti-grind and balance channels and bring logs. Suspected exploits belong in issues with reproductions.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce these standards. They take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, including warnings, temporary interaction limits, and permanent bans for repeated violations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all community spaces, including the repository, issue tracker, discussions, and dashboard community surfaces. It also applies when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement guidelines

1. **Correction.** For minor issues such as inappropriate language or off-topic heat, a private written warning quotes the problematic part.
2. **Warning.** For repeated or more serious issues, a warning states the consequences of continued behavior and applies temporary interaction limits.
3. **Temporary ban.** For serious violations or sustained inappropriate behavior, a temporary ban from community interaction.
4. **Permanent ban.** For patterns of harassment, cheating-exploit abuse, or aggression toward maintainers or members, a permanent ban.

## Reporting

Report suspected violations privately through the private reporting channel configured for this repository (`[private reporting channel]`). Repository owners should replace the placeholder with the channel they maintain.

Do not file conduct reports in public issues, discussions, or the dashboard. Public reports can expose the people involved.

Maintainers review every private report and keep the reporter's identity confidential.
