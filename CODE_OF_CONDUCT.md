# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

This project has a dual audience -- non-coder players/config tuners and ML
researchers -- and both are equally welcome. Beginners asking basic questions
about `world.json`, the dashboard, or training runs receive the same respect
as experienced contributors.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community
* Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Cheating accusations without evidence (use the anti-grind/balance
  channels and bring logs: suspected exploits belong in issues with repros)
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, including warnings,
temporary interaction limits, and permanent bans for repeated violations.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces (repository, issue
tracker, discussions, dashboard community surfaces) and also applies when an
individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement Guidelines

1. **Correction** -- minor issues (inappropriate language, off-topic heat):
   private written warning with the problematic part quoted.
2. **Warning** -- repeated or more serious issues: warning with consequences
   for continued behavior, temporary interaction limits.
3. **Temporary ban** -- serious violations or sustained inappropriate
   behavior: temporary ban from community interaction.
4. **Permanent ban** -- patterns of harassment, cheating-exploit abuse, or
   aggression toward maintainers or members: permanent ban.
